Paper to Poly

Draw a character on paper. Take its photo. Watch it turn into a low-poly 3D model.

  • Use white paper and a dark pen or bright textas.
  • Fill the screen with the paper. No table showing.
  • Join your outline up so the inside gets filled.

Bright part = your character. Rub with your finger to remove bits you don't want.
